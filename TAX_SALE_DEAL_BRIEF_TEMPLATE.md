## I. Capital partner questions

1. Do you consider this exact state/county and this exact sale type (tax lien, tax deed, upset sale, sheriff/foreclosure, REO)?
2. Can you fund the winning bid or required auction deposit by the actual deadline, or do you only consider financing after the bidder acquires title?
3. What proof of funds, deposit source, cash contribution and liquidity verification are required before bidding?
4. What is the shortest realistic underwriting and funding timeline after award?
5. Are funds sent to the auctioneer, county, closing attorney, title company or borrower?
6. Are title cure, redemption, quiet-title, municipal liens or possession issues eligible or excluded?
7. Can rehab be financed? Are draws available, and what inspection/documentation is needed?
8. What borrower experience, credit, liquidity and entity requirements apply?
9. What property types, unit counts, condition levels and exit strategies are eligible?
10. What are the fees, rates, points, minimum/maximum amount, recourse/guaranty and appraisal/valuation requirements?
11. What title, legal, insurance, deed/recording and closing conditions must be met?
12. Is there a documented referral/compensation arrangement, and what disclosures or licensing conditions apply?
